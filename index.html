The Level Up Culture Privacy Policy Last updated: June 8, 2026 Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information The Level Up Culture collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. We've written it to be readable — not just legal. Short version: We collect only what's necessary to run the app. Your journal entries and personal data are private to you. We do not sell your data to anyone, ever. 1. Who We Are The Level Up Culture is a mobile application designed to help beginner gamers build confidence, track their growth, and connect with a supportive community. The app is operated by The Level Up Culture (leahinette04@gmail.com). 2. Information We Collect Account Information When you create an account, we collect: Email address (used for login and account recovery) Username and gamer tag (chosen by you, displayed on your profile) Content You Create When you use the app, we store: Journal entries (title, content, game name, mood, wins, areas for improvement) Daily confidence logs (score and optional note) XP and level progress This content is private to your account. No other user can see it. Device Information If you enable push notifications, we store a push notification token associated with your account to deliver daily reminders. This is optional and can be disabled at any time in your Profile settings. Information We Do Not Collect We do not collect your real name unless you choose to include it in your username We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location We do not track your activity outside of this app We do not use advertising trackers or analytics SDKs 3. How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect solely to: Authenticate your account and keep your data secure Store and display your journal entries, confidence logs, and progress Calculate your XP and level Send daily identity reminder notifications (only if you enable them) Provide account recovery if you forget your password We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond running the app. 4. Data Storage and Security Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted database infrastructure provider. Supabase stores data in secure, encrypted databases with industry-standard access controls. All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption). Row-level security policies ensure that each user can only access their own data — no user can read another user's journal entries or personal information. For more information on Supabase's security practices, visit: supabase.com/security 5. Push Notifications If you grant notification permissions, we use Expo's push notification service to deliver your daily identity reminder. The only data transmitted is your device's push token and the notification content. You can disable notifications at any time through your phone's Settings app or within the Profile tab of the app. 6. Third-Party Services The app uses the following third-party services: Supabase — database and authentication provider. Privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy Expo / Expo Push Notifications — mobile app platform and notification delivery. Privacy policy: expo.dev/privacy We do not integrate with advertising networks, analytics platforms, or social media trackers. 7. Data Retention Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all associated data — including journal entries, confidence logs, and profile information — is permanently deleted from our database within 30 days. To request account deletion, email us at leahinette04@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account" and we will process your request promptly. 8. Children's Privacy The Level Up Culture is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at leahinette04@gmail.com and we will delete that information promptly. 9. Your Rights You have the right to: Access your data — all your journal entries and logs are visible within the app Export your data — contact us to request a data export Correct your data — edit your profile and entries directly in the app Delete your data — delete individual entries in the app, or delete your full account by contacting us Opt out of notifications — disable them in Profile settings or your device Settings at any time 10. Changes to This Policy If we make significant changes to this privacy policy, we will notify you via the app or by email before the changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was most recently changed. 11. Contact Us If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding your privacy or this policy, please reach out: Email: leahinette04@gmail.com Discord: discord.gg/sBR6eYd9J We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 5 business days. © 2026 The Level Up Culture.
